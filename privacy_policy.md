## Privacy Policy

Groundtrack is an open-source app. It has no accounts, no advertising, no
analytics and no crash reporting, and its developer receives nothing from it.

**What stays on the phone**

Flight logs, video recordings, settings and the map and terrain cache are kept
on the phone. Flight logs include the phone's own position, so a replay can
show where the operator stood. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you export or
share it yourself.

**What the app fetches**

To draw what is on screen, the app downloads map and terrain tiles for the area
being viewed:

*   map tiles from [OpenStreetMap](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy) and [OpenTopoMap](https://opentopomap.org/about)
*   satellite imagery from [Esri](https://www.esri.com/en-us/privacy/overview)
*   terrain heights from the public elevation tiles on [Amazon Web Services](https://aws.amazon.com/privacy/)

These services see the tiles requested, and so roughly where the map is
looking, along with the phone's IP address.

**Only when you turn them on, or tap them**

*   Nearby aircraft: the area around the model is sent to
    [FlightRadar24](https://www.flightradar24.com/privacy-policy) to ask what is flying there.
*   Drone GPS as phone location: the model's position is handed to the phone
    as its own location, which other apps on the phone then receive.
*   Find my quad: a route or a shared message opens your maps or messaging app
    with the model's last position.
